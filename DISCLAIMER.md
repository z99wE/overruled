# Disclaimer

overrruled is an **entertainment and educational tool**, not legal advice, and
not a substitute for licensed counsel.

- The judgments, statutes, and case summaries in the corpus are distilled for
  gameplay. They may be outdated, oversimplified, or rendered at the wrong
  granularity for your matter.
- "Verified in corpus" means the cited card exists in the bundled reference
  text — it is not a guarantee that the citation is good law in your
  jurisdiction or for your facts.
- The LLM is a stochastic system. Its outputs can be wrong, confidently
  wrong, or outright hallucinated. It cannot think, judge, or practice law.
- Nothing the Bench, Co-Counsel, or opposing agents say is a preview of any
  real court's ruling.

For real legal matters, consult a qualified attorney licensed in your
jurisdiction. Do not rely on anything in this application for legal decisions.
